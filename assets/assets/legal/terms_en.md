# Terms of use

**Draft of 23 October 2026, subject to legal review before launch. The Arabic version is binding.**

## 1. About the platform
Patient Access keeps each patient's medical file, lets registered clinics and hospitals see it with the patient's permission, and lets them write e-prescriptions that registered pharmacies dispense once.

## 2. Accounts and verification
- Account holders must be 18 or older and use their real details as on their national ID.
- The mobile number is confirmed by a WhatsApp message. One account per national ID and per mobile.
- Our team checks doctors, nurses and pharmacists, including their syndicate registration, before activation.
- A parent manages files for children under 18. At 18 the child can join with their national ID and take over the file.

## 3. Access to a file
- No clinic or hospital sees a file without a code or QR from the patient. A code works once within 10 minutes.
- The patient can take access away at any time. What the facility recorded before stays in the file.
- Every opening and every new record is logged, and the patient can see the log.

## 4. Medical records
- Records are permanent and cannot be edited or deleted. Mistakes are fixed with a new, linked correction.
- Doctors are responsible for what they write. The platform is a recording and communication tool and gives no medical advice.
- What patients add themselves is labelled "Added by the patient".

## 5. E-prescriptions
- Each prescription is dispensed once, within its validity. The doctor can cancel it before it is dispensed.
- The pharmacy sees the medicines, doses, and the patient's name and age only.
- Prescriptions with controlled (table) drugs are valid for fewer days, and the pharmacist checks the last 4 digits of the patient's national ID. Both sides must follow the laws on these drugs.

## 6. Fees
Free for patients and pharmacies. Clinics and hospitals pay a yearly subscription (to be announced) after a free trial.

## 7. Not allowed
Impersonation, opening files without permission, false data, or trying to get around the protections. We may suspend such accounts.

## 8. Emergencies
This is not an emergency service. In an emergency call 123.

## 9. Law
These terms follow the laws of Egypt, including the Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020.
