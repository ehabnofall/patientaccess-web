# Privacy policy

**Draft of 23 October 2026, subject to legal review before launch. The Arabic version is binding.**

## 1. What we collect
- **Identity:** name, national ID, birth date, sex, governorate, address, email, mobile; syndicate number for clinicians and pharmacists.
- **Health data (sensitive):** history, allergies, chronic illnesses, medicines, visits, vital signs, prescriptions.
- **Activity log:** who opened or added to a file, and when.

## 2. Why
To run the medical file, let facilities the patient chooses see it, dispense prescriptions, and verify users. We do not use the data for advertising and never sell it.

## 3. Consent
Health data is processed on the explicit consent given at sign-up, which can be withdrawn as the law allows.

## 4. Who sees what
- The patient, or a parent for children under 18.
- Doctors and nurses at facilities the patient allowed, while the permission lasts.
- The pharmacy shown a prescription: medicines, doses, patient name and age only.
- Our team sees verification data only, never medical records.

## 5. Protection
Strict database access rules, encryption in transit, automatic sign-out after 15 minutes of inactivity, and breach notification within the legal deadlines.

## 6. Where data is stored
Currently with a cloud provider in the EU (Frankfurt). Before storing real patients' data we will obtain the required permits from the Personal Data Protection Centre, or move hosting as the law requires.

## 7. Your rights
Access, a copy, correction, withdrawal of consent, and account deletion within legal limits (medical records must be kept for a set period).

## 8. Contact
Contact us at the email shown on the site.
